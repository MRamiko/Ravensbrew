How a batch actually goes

Five stages, tracked in every roast log.

0:00 — 4:30 · 100°C

Drying Phase

Green beans lose moisture and turn from grassy to pale yellow. No flavor develops yet — this is pure preparation.

4:30 — 8:30 · 150–190°C

Maillard Reaction

Sugars and amino acids brown the bean and build the base of its eventual flavor — bread crust, toasted grain.

8:30 — 9:20 · 196°C

First Crack

Beans audibly pop as internal steam pressure releases. This is where light roasts are pulled.

9:20 — 11:00 · 205–218°C

Development

The stretch between cracks, where we decide how much acidity to keep versus how much body to build.

11:00 — 11:40 · 224°C

Second Crack & Drop

A quieter, higher-pitched crack signals oils reaching the surface. We drop here for our dark roasts.
