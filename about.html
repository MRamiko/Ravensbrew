Our story

Roasting is a craft. We treat it like one.

How it started

From a garage roaster to a full roastery.

Ravensbrew began in 2018 when three friends pooled savings for a secondhand 6kg drum roaster and started selling bags at the weekend farmers market. Word spread faster than the roaster could keep up, and within a year we'd moved into our current space with a proper 12kg machine.

What hasn't changed is how we buy: in person, from growers we return to season after season, at prices we settle on together rather than dictate.

Milestones

Eight years, one roaster.

  • 2018

    First bags sold at the farmers market

    Roasting on a 6kg drum out of a converted garage, three days a week.

  • 2019

    Moved into the current roastery

    Upgraded to a 12kg drum and opened a small retail counter.

  • 2021

    First direct-trade farm visit

    Traveled to Huila, Colombia to meet our first direct-trade grower partner.

  • 2024

    Started wholesale partnerships

    Began supplying beans to independent cafés across the region.

  • 2026

    Launched online subscriptions

    Made it possible to get a fresh bag shipped every two weeks, anywhere.

What we stand for

Three things we won't compromise on.

01 — Traceability

Every bag lists the farm, the altitude, and the processing method.

If we can't trace it back to a specific lot, we don't buy it — no blends of unknown origin, ever.

02 — Freshness

Nothing spends more than two weeks between drum and cup.

We roast to order in batches under 12kg, tracked against the same curve you saw on the homepage.

03 — Fair Pricing

We pay above Fair Trade minimums, negotiated in person each season.

Prices are set directly with growers we return to, not through a commodity exchange.
